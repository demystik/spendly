Spendly Account & Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: June 2026

Spendly allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Spendly account and associated data, please email:

YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS

Subject: Account Deletion Request – Spendly

Please include the email address associated with your Spendly account.

Alternatively, if an in-app delete option becomes available, users may delete their account directly from the app settings.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your request is processed, we will permanently delete:

  • Your Spendly account
  • Authentication information
  • Expense records
  • Budget information
  • Income information
  • User profile data stored in Firebase

Data That May Be Retained

We may temporarily retain limited technical records required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security monitoring

Such retained information, if applicable, will be deleted within 30 days after account deletion processing.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are usually processed within 7 business days.
